What is GDG Fest?

GDG Fest is a community-run developer festival built around three simple ideas: learn something new, build something real and meet people who love technology.

Expect a mix of talks, hands-on workshops and a hackathon, with space to ask questions and find mentors. Specific details will be shared by the organizers once they are confirmed.
